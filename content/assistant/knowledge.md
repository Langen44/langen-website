## The company
- Langen Motorcycles (Langen Technology Ltd) was founded in 2018 by Christofer Ratcliffe, formerly Chief Design Engineer at CCM.
- Workshop: Unit 1, Kingfisher Court, South Lancs Industrial Estate, Wigan WN4 9DW, beside the Three Sisters circuit, two minutes from junction 25 of the M6. Visits by appointment and always welcome.
- Contact: info@langenmotorcycles.co.uk, 01942 724 059. Every enquiry is answered personally by Christofer or one of the team, usually within a working day.
- The team came from CCM, Triumph, BAC, McLaren and M-Sport. There are no dealers: owners deal with the factory directly, and the engineers who build a bike look after it.
- Highly Commended, Small Business of the Year, Wigan Borough Business Awards 2026.

## The Lightspeed
- 1,190cc liquid-cooled 72º V-twin developed by Rotax and built by Buell, with Langen's own injection and electronics. 185 hp at 10,600 rpm, 138 Nm at 8,200 rpm. 185 kg dry. Over 1,000 bhp per tonne.
- Limited to 185 numbered builds worldwide; 50 are allocated to the United States.
- Full carbon bodywork, high-tensile steel tubular space frame, Langen forged aluminium wheels (3.5 x 17 front, 8.0 x 17 rear), Öhlins FGRT 301 forks with superbike cartridge kit, twin Öhlins STX piggyback shocks, Langen/HEL brakes with 320 mm front discs and cornering ABS, 6-speed gearbox with slipper clutch, 790 mm seat, 19-litre tank, CNC-machined dash with TFT touchscreen.
- Rider aids developed in house: traction, wheelie, launch and cruise control.
- Top speed is geared to around 150 mph as standard, depending on riding mode; gearing can be changed to suit the owner.
- Price: from £37,000 plus VAT for a complete, finished motorcycle. The standard colourway is silver over a bronze chassis with champagne wheels, included in the price. A heavily specified bike with full custom paint, the K-Tech suspension pack, upgraded wheels and bespoke finishes can reach around £50,000; most builds land between the two.
- Reservation: a £1,000 deposit secures a numbered build. It is fully refundable until the specification session is complete, the sales agreement is signed and manufacturing begins. The deposit comes off the price of the bike.
- Delivery: UK-specification Lightspeeds deliver first, in build order. Fully homologated versions for the USA, Europe, Japan, the Middle East and Australasia follow through 2027. US riders who would rather not wait can take an early UK-specification build and register it through the Montana route.
- Export: priced without UK VAT. Air freight door to door, crated and insured, is typically around £3,000; duty and local taxes are paid on arrival. The team will put together a landed figure for any country or US state on request.
- Commissioning: a day at the workshop with the design engineers (or a video call with the bike in front of us for overseas owners); fitting of seat, bars, footrests, ride height and suspension to the rider; specification of paint, finishes, materials and hard parts; the build by hand; handover with certificate and build record.
- LS12 Lightspeed Turbo: twelve forced-induction Lightspeeds drawn from the 185, offered first to Lightspeed build-number holders and Two Stroke owners. Details on application.

## The Two Stroke
- 249.5cc 90º two-stroke V-twin developed with Vins Motors in Maranello, with Langen's patented electronic injection. 76 hp, 45 Nm, power band from 9,500 rpm, redline 12,700 rpm. 120 kg. 7020-T6 aluminium space frame, carbon fibre tank and bodywork, Öhlins 43 mm forks, twin K-Tech Razor shocks, Brembo discs with HEL billet calipers.
- One hundred were built between 2019 and 2025. All were sold.
- Certified Pre-Owned: when an owner passes theirs on it returns through the factory, is inspected and prepared, receives a new Certificate of Ownership and a minimum twelve-month warranty, and is offered to people who have registered interest first. Pre-owned bikes carry no VAT in the UK or US; shipping, duty and local taxes apply for export.
- From time to time a build allocation is released by a client unable to proceed; these are offered from £32,500 plus VAT and completed to the new owner's specification.

## Owners
- Owners have a direct line to the factory and to Christofer. Overseas owners appoint a workshop of their choice, which Langen equips with documents, tools and a direct line to the engineers.
- The owners' portal on the site holds each bike's documents, build record and videos; sign in with model, build number and the password set with the activation code supplied with the bike.
- Future ultra-limited editions are offered to owners and build-number holders before anyone else.
