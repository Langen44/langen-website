We are delighted to have picked up the Highly Commended award for Small Business of the Year at the Wigan Borough Business Awards. It is a testament to the team's relentless work and uncompromising attitude to our bikes, our business and the journey.

## What the judges said

> The judges were particularly impressed by the ambition behind Langen Motorcycles and its determination to compete at the very highest level despite operating with a relatively small team. Its success has been driven by an uncompromising focus on quality, innovation and attention to detail, alongside significant reinvestment in the future of the business. With an increasingly international customer base and ambitious plans for continued growth, Langen Motorcycles is demonstrating that you don't have to be the biggest to compete on a global stage.

Thank you to everyone involved in organising a wonderful and successful event, and congratulations to GMS Services Ltd on first place – very well deserved – and to all the other finalists.

Now, back to work.
